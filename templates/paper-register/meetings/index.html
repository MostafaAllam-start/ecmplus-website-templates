MMS · Meeting Management

Committee work runs from agenda to signed minutes, with every action tracked.

ECM+ schedules meetings in person or online, records votes on each agenda item, sends the minutes for digital signature and follows every action item.

01 Overview

Every meeting documented from start to finish

Meetings are planned on a shared calendar, with rooms, clash detection and invitations in each invitee’s language.

During the meeting, attendance, notes and votes are recorded, and the minutes are prepared from the meeting itself.

02 Capabilities

Key capabilities

  • Planning

    Month, week and day calendars, rooms and buildings with clash detection, and a warning when the organizer is double-booked.

  • In person and online

    Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Zoom, Beem or ECM+ Meet hosted by your organization, with Google Calendar synchronization.

  • Bilingual invitations

    Invitations are sent in Arabic, English or both, or in each invitee’s language.

  • During the session

    Attendance, agenda notes and chat are recorded during the meeting.

  • Voting

    Single or multiple choice per agenda item. Results stay hidden until a member votes or the vote closes, and a closed vote is final.

  • Minutes and digital signature

    Minutes are prepared from the meeting, presented to members for signature with progress tracking and reminders, then distributed and archived.

  • Action items

    Each action has an assignee, a due date, a priority and reminders, with a daily digest.

  • Follow-up meetings

    Follow-up meetings stay linked to the meeting they continue.

03 How it works

How a meeting is run

  1. 01

    Plan

    The room is booked, clashes are checked and the invitations are sent.

  2. 02

    Meet

    The meeting is held in person or online, and attendance is recorded.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Members vote on each agenda item.

  4. 04

    Sign

    The minutes are presented to members for digital signature and tracked until every member signs.

  5. 05

    Follow up

    Action items are assigned and tracked to completion.
