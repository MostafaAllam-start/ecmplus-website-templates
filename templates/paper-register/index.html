02 Lifecycle

    Capture

    Route

03 Governance

Every view and every download is traceable.

Search reaches the full content of each file, down to the page. Every protected view and download is recorded and can be traced.

  • Full-text search and search by meaning

    Results are highlighted down to the page. Search works by meaning, by field or by voice.

  • Text recognition and AI auto-fill

    The “Auto fill from file” function reads scanned and digital documents and proposes their metadata.

  • Access trace code (forensic watermark)

    Each protected view or download carries a unique access trace code that identifies who opened it and when. 17 types of access are traced.

  • Locked documents

    Locked documents open only with a one-time verification code sent by email, SMS or WhatsApp. Their content remains hidden in search and reports.

  • Office add-ins and web editing

    Documents can be edited in the built-in ECM+ Editor, or in Word, Excel and PowerPoint without leaving ECM+. The Outlook add-in saves and routes emails.

  • Report Builder

    The drag-and-drop designer supports cover pages, headers and footers. Reports are exported to PDF, Word or Excel.

  • Hijri calendar and Arabic interface

    The suite provides Arabic and English interfaces with full right-to-left layout, Hijri and Gregorian calendars, and bilingual emails, forms and reports.

  • WhatsApp, SMS and email

    One-time verification codes are sent by WhatsApp, SMS or email. Two-factor authentication codes and process notifications are sent by WhatsApp or email.

  • Nafath sign-in

    Users can sign in with Nafath, the Saudi national identity service, on its own or alongside passwords.
