BPM · Business Process Management

Processes are designed visually and run with deadlines and automatic escalation.

ECM+ turns your organization’s procedures into processes with forms, assigned tasks and time limits, and shows where every request stands.

Request a demoExplore the suite

01Overview

From a documented procedure to a running process

A visual designer defines the steps, decisions, parallel paths and timers of each process.

Forms collect the data, tasks reach the right people, and each step escalates automatically when its deadline passes.

02Capabilities

Key capabilities

  • Visual process designer

    Steps, decisions, parallel paths and timers. Each process can be tested step by step before it goes live.

  • 7 kinds of steps

    Tasks for people, automatic steps, data exchange with other systems, data from existing databases, notifications, archiving and PDF documents.

  • Form builder

    Drag-and-drop fields, signature fields, conditional fields, multi-page forms, and layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop.

  • Task assignment

    Tasks go to a person, department, group, manager or someone named in the form, with shared task pools.

  • Deadlines and escalation

    Each step has a time limit, with automatic escalation when it is exceeded.

  • Versioned processes

    Processes are drafted, published, retired and restored, and each one has its own list of who may start it.

  • Oversight

    Administrators find stuck or failed steps, retry them, and pause, resume or stop a process.

  • Starter templates

    Approval, leave request, employee onboarding, order fulfilment and applicant processing.

03How it works

From design to a running process

  1. 01

    Design

    The steps, decisions and timers are defined in the visual designer.

  2. 02

    Build the forms

    The form builder creates the forms that each step needs.

  3. 03

    Test

    The process runs step by step before it is published.

  4. 04

    Run

    Requests move through the steps, and tasks reach the assigned people.

  5. 05

    Monitor

    Tasks escalate automatically when their deadlines pass, and administrators resolve stuck steps.
