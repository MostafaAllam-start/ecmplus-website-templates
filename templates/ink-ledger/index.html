02Security

Every view and download is traceable.

Each protected view or download carries a unique access trace code. Scanning the code on a leaked page identifies the user, the time, the channel, the device and the network address.

Enforcement mode

  • Off
  • Trace only
  • Enforced

Configured per module, with exemptions where required.

  • Audit trail

    Every addition, view, edit, download, print, share, deletion and lock is recorded.

  • Two-factor authentication

    Verification codes are sent by WhatsApp or email.

  • Nafath sign-in

    Sign-in with the Saudi national identity, alone or together with a password.

  • Digital signatures

    Employees sign digitally, and forms and minutes accept drawn or saved signatures.

  • Expiring shared links

    Links to previews and files expire automatically.

  • Backups

    Scheduled backups and named snapshots can be restored, and old copies are removed on schedule.

03Solutions

Example scenarios by type of organization.

The following scenarios illustrate how organizations use the suite. They are examples, not customer case studies.

Example scenario · Municipalities

Incoming requests are registered, reviewed and answered officially.

An incoming correspondence is registered with a serial number and Hijri and Gregorian dates, then routed to the responsible department. The reply is reviewed and approved, and the Outgoing Officer prints, stamps and dispatches it. The correspondence graph records every step.

  • Correspondence
  • Documents
  1. 01RegistrationThe serial number, parties, priority and due date are recorded.
  2. 02RoutingThe correspondence is routed to employees, groups or departments as To, CC or BCC.
  3. 03ReviewThe reply is sent for review, then approved or returned.
  4. 04DispatchThe reply is printed, stamped and delivered, then archived with its full record.

Example scenario · Ministries & authorities

Every access to a confidential file is recorded and attributable.

Employees sign in with Nafath or two-factor authentication. Confidential correspondence carries its sensitivity and priority. Locked documents open only with a one-time verification code, and every protected view or download carries an access trace code.

  • Correspondence
  • Documents
  • Security
  1. 01Sign-inNafath, or a password with a code sent by WhatsApp or email.
  2. 02ClassificationThe sensitivity, priority and structured metadata are recorded.
  3. 03LockingLocked documents open with a one-time verification code sent by email, SMS or WhatsApp, and stay hidden in search.
  4. 04TracingEvery protected view carries an access trace code and is recorded in the audit trail.

Example scenario · Committees

Committee meetings are managed from agenda to signed minutes.

The committee secretary books a room with clash detection and invites members in their preferred language. The agenda runs with a session timer, members vote on each item, and the minutes are sent for signature. Action items are then tracked to completion.

  • Meetings
  1. 01SchedulingRooms and buildings are booked, with Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Zoom, ECM+ Meet or Beem.
  2. 02SessionThe session timer, attendance and agenda notes are recorded.
  3. 03VotingResults remain hidden until a member votes or the vote closes; a closed vote is final.
  4. 04Signature and follow-upSigning progress is tracked, and action items are assigned with reminders.

Example scenario · Records & archive teams

Legacy archives are migrated, classified and searchable to the page.

The records team migrates the legacy archive through structured imports that resume where they stopped and can be rolled back. Files are classified into document types with defined fields, and text recognition proposes metadata. Retention rules delete or notify on schedule, and search covers the content of every file.

  • Documents
  1. 01MigrationStructured imports resume where they stopped and can be rolled back.
  2. 02ClassificationDocument types, defined fields and “Auto fill from file” are applied.
  3. 03SearchSearch by keyword or by meaning, with results highlighted to the page.
  4. 04RetentionScheduled Delete or Notify actions run, with an execution log.

Frequently asked questions

Can ECM+ run on our own servers?

Yes. ECM+ is installed on your organization’s servers, in your own data centre or in the cloud, so your documents remain under your control.

Is Arabic fully supported?

Yes. The suite provides Arabic and English interfaces, right-to-left layouts, Hijri and Gregorian calendars, and bilingual emails, forms and reports.

Can we start with one module?

Yes. Each module is licensed separately, and the other modules remain inactive until required.

Does ECM+ work with our existing systems?

Yes. Each connection receives only the access your organization grants. Processes exchange data with your databases and business systems, the organization structure stays in sync with HR, and Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook work with ECM+ directly.

Can we bring our existing archive?

Yes. You can move your existing archive from legacy systems into ECM+ through structured imports. If an import is interrupted, it resumes from where it stopped, and it can be rolled back if needed.
