Overview

Official correspondence at every stage

Each correspondence carries a serial number, external references, parties, priority, sensitivity, a due date and tags.

Review and approval take place before anything leaves the organization, and the correspondence graph shows the path of each item.

Capabilities

Key capabilities

  • 3 directions

    Incoming, outgoing and internal correspondence, with serial numbers and external references.

  • Routing to anyone

    Correspondence is routed to employees, groups, departments or external parties, as To, CC or BCC.

  • Instructions in any form

    Instructions are attached as text, a file, a voice note or a video note.

  • Review and approval

    Correspondence is sent for review, then approved or returned before dispatch.

  • External dispatch

    The Outgoing Officer prints, stamps and dispatches approved correspondence.

  • Correspondence graph

    A visual map shows where each correspondence went, with a log of every action.

  • Routing rules

    Rules forward correspondence automatically by trigger, conditions and recipients.

  • Mail inbox

    Gmail, Outlook or a business email account connects to the suite, and emails are filed into a correspondence or the archive.

How it works

How a correspondence is handled

  1. 01

    Register

    The correspondence receives a serial number, its parties and its due date.

  2. 02

    Route

    It is routed to the responsible employees or departments, with instructions.

  3. 03

    Review

    The outgoing correspondence is reviewed, then approved or returned.

  4. 04

    Dispatch

    The Outgoing Officer prints, stamps and dispatches the approved correspondence.

  5. 05

    Archive

    The correspondence is closed and archived with its full record.
