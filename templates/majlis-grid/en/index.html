المراسلات · CMS

Correspondence Management

A recorded route for every item of correspondence and everyone who handled it.

  • Incoming, outgoing and internal correspondence, with serial numbers and external references.
  • Review before dispatch: sent to review, approved or returned, then dispatched.
  • A visual map of each correspondence route, and a log of every action taken on it.

سير العمليات · BPM

Business Process Management

Process automation with a visual designer and set deadlines.

  • A visual process designer that tests each process step by step before it goes live.
  • A drag-and-drop form builder with conditional fields and signature fields.
  • A deadline for every step, with automatic escalation when it is exceeded.

الاجتماعات · MMS

Meeting Management

Meetings managed from the agenda to the signed minutes.

  • In-person and online meetings through Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Zoom, Beem or ECM+ Meet.
  • Voting on each agenda item; results remain hidden until a member votes or the vote closes, and a closed vote is final.
  • Meeting minutes are sent for signature, and action items are tracked to completion.

Security

The suite includes a forensic watermark, an audit trail, digital signatures and backups. These controls cover every stage, from sign-in to printing.

Processes

Processes are designed visually and run within set deadlines

The visual designer lets your teams build and test each process step by step before it goes live. It supports 7 types of steps, and tasks escalate automatically when their deadline is exceeded.
