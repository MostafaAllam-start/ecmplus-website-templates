Support hand-off

Support links never point at the portal directly. They hit /support/*, and a controller redirects to the ticketing portal.

LinkPortal
/support/{lang}
/support/login/{lang}/login
/support/tickets/new/{lang}/tickets
/support/tickets/{lang}/tickets
/support/suggestions/{lang}/suggestions
