# Portico image briefs (for AI generation or a commissioned shoot)

`p01` to `p05` and `p10` are AI-generated; the rest are Unsplash placeholders (see `CREDITS.md`). To replace one, generate
a landscape image that follows its brief below and export it under the same name at 800, 1200
and 1600 px wide as WebP (quality about 60; keep the 1600 px file near 250 KB or less). Keep the proportions close to the current file
so the layout does not shift.

**Shared direction for every image**
- Calm, institutional, premium. Natural or soft architectural light; limestone, warm white,
  graphite, brushed metal and deep navy tones; a restrained warm gold accent at most.
- No green or teal cast. No legible text, logos, flags, screens with readable content,
  or identifiable faces. No people unless the brief says so, and then only out of focus or
  from behind.
- Photographic realism, 35 mm or 24 mm lens look, no fisheye, no heavy HDR or vignette.
- Leave quiet negative space where the brief says a text plate sits over the image.
